Research track A

Quantum gravity & objective collapse

Penrose–Hameroff Orchestrated Objective Reduction (Orch-OR) and the Diósi–Penrose model of gravitationally-induced wavefunction collapse on a timescale τ ≈ ℏ / EG ⚠ Contested hypothesis — rigor, not advocacy

Reviews & foundational papers

Start here for the state of the field and the origin of the collapse-time proposal.

  • Models of Wave-function Collapse, Underlying Theories, and Experimental Tests ↗
    Bassi, Lochan, Satin, Singh & Ulbricht · 2013 · Rev. Mod. Phys. 85, 471 arXiv:1204.4325

    The definitive review of collapse models — including gravity-induced collapse and its experimental tests.

  • On Gravity's Role in Quantum State Reduction
    R. Penrose · 1996 · Gen. Relativ. Gravit. 28, 581 journal · pre-arXiv

    The original argument for a gravitationally-induced collapse time τ ≈ ℏ/EG.

  • Models for universal reduction of macroscopic quantum fluctuations
    L. Diósi · 1989 · Phys. Rev. A 40, 1165 journal · pre-arXiv

    The earlier decay-time proposal that, with Penrose's, defines the "Diósi–Penrose" model.

  • Consciousness in the universe: A review of the 'Orch OR' theory
    S. Hameroff & R. Penrose · 2014 · Phys. Life Rev. 11, 39 journal

    The biological Orch-OR hypothesis (microtubules) — the specific contested claim Track A stress-tests.

Key experiments & recent work

Where the model meets data — and where it fails.

  • Underground test of gravity-related wave function collapse ↗
    Donadi, Piscicchia, Curceanu, Diósi, Laubenstein & Bassi · 2021 · Nature Physics 17, 74 arXiv:2111.13490

    Gran Sasso spontaneous-radiation measurement that rules out the parameter-free Diósi–Penrose model.

  • On the effectiveness of the collapse in the Diósi–Penrose model ↗
    2024 arXiv:2406.18494

    Recent scrutiny of whether the DP mechanism actually localizes macroscopic superpositions.

  • Gravity and the Collapse of the Wave Function: a Probe into the Diósi–Penrose model ↗
    2014 arXiv:1408.6460

    Analysis of the cut-off dependence and falsifiable regimes of the model.

  • Gravity-induced wave-function collapse in a temporally expanding spacetime ↗
    2017 arXiv:1701.01765

    Collapse dynamics in a cosmological setting.

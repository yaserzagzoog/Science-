## About Us

Zagzoog for Home Appliances was founded in 1959 by Sheikh Suleiman Said Zagzoog (May Allah have mercy on his soul).

The company has great experience in the marketing and sale of home appliances for more than 65 years. Zagzoog has 20 branches, 84 showrooms, 20 customer service centers and more than 200 distributors, and the company's products have been shown in more than 500 exhibitions across various regions of the Kingdom, providing the best services for all customers — from supply and installation to after-sales service — in order to maintain customer loyalty and satisfaction.

Zagzoog also has extensive experience in the marketing and installation of air-conditioners, and owns private companies and factories that manufacture ductwork products and handle design and décor.

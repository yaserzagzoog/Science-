## 🔬 Research (arXiv)

**14 recent arXiv papers** on **🔬 Longevity Research** — aging biology, senescence & lifespan (`q-bio`). Fetched 9 Jul 2026 · newest first.

> Note: arXiv's quantitative-biology corpus on aging is smaller than the physics fields, and its `q-bio.PE` category mixes in epidemic / age-structured population models that merely use "age" in a demographic sense. This list is **curated to genuinely aging/longevity-focused work**; pure epidemiology and vaccination-strategy papers were filtered out.

---

### [Control Laws in Aging and Longevity: A Control Theory of Aging for Gerotherapeutic Drug Discovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16781)

**Authors:** Alex Zhavoronkov, Bud Mishra · **Published:** 2026-05-16

A control-theoretic framework defining aging as loss of safe controllability, with prescriptive intervention targeting for drug discovery.

### [Demographic Senescence as Multi-Level Selection in Miniature](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00262)

**Authors:** Ananda Shikhara Bhat, Hanna Kokko · **Published:** 2026-06-30

Shows aging mortality patterns emerge mathematically equivalent to multi-level selection in structured populations.

### [A Conceptual Model for the Evo-Devo Role of Transposable Elements and Its Implications for the Ageing Phenomenon](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31733)

**Authors:** Alessandro Fontana · **Published:** 2026-06-30

Proposes that age-related release of epigenetic repression of transposable elements drives both regulatory innovation and aging.

### [Evolutionary Entropy Shapes Reproductive Lifespan in Age-Structured Populations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22001)

**Authors:** Jorge Buescu, Saber Elaydi, Henrique Oliveira · **Published:** 2026-06-20

Reproductive windows across species organize near entropy-maximizing age classes, validated across 130 species.

### [Sensitivity of Evolutionary Entropy in Lefkovitch Matrices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26170)

**Authors:** Henrique M. Oliveira · **Published:** 2026-06-24

A complete sensitivity theory for evolutionary entropy in stage-structured populations, with closed-form demographic expressions.

### [Aging-Induced Structural Alterations in SR-Mitochondria Interaction in Skeletal Muscle](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17457)

**Authors:** Unmod Senapati, Barsha Priyadarshini Kar, Sunil Pani, Naresh Chandra Bal · **Published:** 2026-06-16

Reviews how aging disrupts mitochondria–sarcoplasmic-reticulum contacts critical for muscle calcium signaling and energy metabolism.

### [What is Life?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15905)

**Authors:** Roger D. Jones · **Published:** 2026-06-14

Six postulates for adaptive living systems combining entropy, information longevity, environmental response, and molecular switches.

### [A Senescent-Immune Reserve Niche Model for Incomplete Lobular Involution in the Aging Breast](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13902)

**Authors:** Jaida C. Lue, Darren J. Baker, Amy C. Degnim, Stacey J. Winham, Mark E. Sherman, Derek C. Radisky · **Published:** 2026-05-12

Proposes persistent aging breast lobules are maintained by an active senescent-immune niche rather than passive hormonal regression.

### [A Putative Model of the Gut-Muscle Axis in Aged Livestock](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19171)

**Authors:** Karin Suzuki, Aoi Fukushima, Yu Adachi, et al. · **Published:** 2026-05-18

Multi-omics analysis linking gut microbiota and metabolites to muscle protein metabolism in aged laying hens.

### [Subcortical Shape Variations and Their Associations with Cognition Across the 8th Decade of Life (Lothian Birth Cohort 1936)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29703)

**Authors:** Maria del C. Valdes-Hernandez, Wonjung Park, Joanna Moodie, et al. · **Published:** 2026-05-28

Longitudinal neuroimaging links heterogeneous subcortical brain changes to cognitive decline in older adults.

### [Stability Analysis and Long-Time Convergence of a PDE Model of Two-Phase Ageing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19814)

**Authors:** Luce Breuil · **Published:** 2026-03-20

Demonstrates global stability of an age-structured population model with distinct aging phases and competition.

### [Branching Random Walks with Ageing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22783)

**Authors:** Daniela Bertacchi, Elena Montanaro, Fabio Zucca · **Published:** 2026-02-26

Models how reproductive capacity declining with age affects population dynamics in branching processes.

### [Artificial Intelligence Applications in Parkinson's Disease via Retinal Imaging](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12281)

**Authors:** Ali Jafarizadeh, Hamidreza Ashayeri, Hadi Vahedi, et al. · **Published:** 2026-03-04

AI-enabled retinal imaging achieves high accuracy for early Parkinson's detection, relevant to aging neurodegeneration.

### [Face Morphometric Profiles of Groups as Early Markers for Certain Diseases?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20103)

**Authors:** Roberto Herrero, Yoanna Martinez-Diaz, Heydi Mendez-Vazquez, Joan Nieves, Augusto Gonzalez · **Published:** 2026-05-19

Population-level facial morphometry proposes genetic facial traits as risk markers for Alzheimer's and other age-related conditions.

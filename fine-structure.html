Research track B

The fine-structure constant

Is the electromagnetic coupling α = e² / (4πε₀ℏc) ≈ 1 / 137.036 constant across space and time? A recognized open question tested with quasar-absorption spectra and laboratory spectroscopy.

Reviews & foundational papers

The framework for varying fundamental constants and their observational status.

  • Varying Constants, Gravitation and Cosmology ↗
    J.-P. Uzan · 2011 · Living Rev. Relativ. 14, 2 arXiv:1009.5514

    The comprehensive review: what varying constants would mean, and every observational constraint (clocks, Oklo, quasars, CMB, BBN).

Key measurements & analyses

Quasar-absorption results and the debate over systematics.

  • Indications of a spatial variation of the fine structure constant ↗
    Webb, King, Murphy, Flambaum, Carswell & Bainbridge · 2011 · PRL 107, 191101 arXiv:1008.3907

    The "Australian dipole": Keck + VLT data fit a spatial variation in α at the 4.2σ level.

  • Time evolution of the fine structure constant ↗
    2002 arXiv:astro-ph/0209488

    Early Keck/HIRES evidence for a smaller α in the past across many absorption systems.

  • Searching for variations in the fine-structure constant and the proton-to-electron mass ratio using quasar absorption lines ↗
    2012 arXiv:1202.6365

    Method and joint constraints on α and μ = mp/me.

  • On the Evidence for Cosmic Variation of the Fine Structure Constant (I): A Parametric Bayesian Model Selection Analysis of the Quasar Dataset ↗
    2012 arXiv:1207.6223

    A statistically careful re-analysis — essential for judging how strong the dipole evidence really is.

  • High-precision limit on variation in the fine-structure constant from a single quasar absorption system ↗
    Kotus, Murphy & Carswell · 2017 arXiv:1609.03860

    A tight single-system constraint using improved wavelength calibration — a model for systematics control.

  • Constraining the Variation of the Fine Structure Constant with Observations of Narrow Quasar Absorption Lines ↗
    2014 arXiv:1406.3628

    Complementary constraints from narrow absorption lines.

Research track C

Dark matter

The missing-mass problem — flat rotation curves flat v(r) ⇒ M(r) ∝ r · Ωdm ≈ 0.27 weighed against particle candidates and modified-dynamics alternatives using rotation curves and gravitational lensing.

Reviews & foundational papers

Evidence, candidates, and the history of the problem.

  • Particle Dark Matter: Evidence, Candidates and Constraints ↗
    Bertone, Hooper & Silk · 2005 · Phys. Rept. 405, 279 arXiv:hep-ph/0404175

    The classic wide-scope review of the evidence and the particle-candidate landscape.

  • Dark Matter Candidates from Particle Physics and Methods of Detection ↗
    J. L. Feng · 2010 · Ann. Rev. Astron. Astrophys. 48, 495 arXiv:1003.0904

    Candidate taxonomy — WIMPs, axions, sterile neutrinos, superWIMPs — and how each would be detected.

  • A History of Dark Matter ↗
    Bertone & Hooper · 2018 · Rev. Mod. Phys. 90, 045002 arXiv:1605.04909

    How dark matter became part of the standard cosmological model — useful context for hypothesis triage.

Key evidence & datasets

The observations that discriminate between models.

  • A direct empirical proof of the existence of dark matter ↗
    Clowe et al. · 2006 · ApJL 648, L109 arXiv:astro-ph/0608407

    The Bullet Cluster: an 8σ offset between mass (lensing) and baryons (X-ray) that challenges pure modified-gravity accounts.

  • SPARC: Mass Models for 175 Disk Galaxies with Spitzer Photometry and Accurate Rotation Curves ↗
    Lelli, McGaugh & Schombert · 2016 · AJ 152, 157 arXiv:1606.09251

    The benchmark rotation-curve dataset for testing dark-matter and modified-dynamics models.

  • Planck 2018 results. VI. Cosmological parameters ↗
    Planck Collaboration · 2020 · A&A 641, A6 arXiv:1807.06209

    Sets the cosmic budget: cold dark matter density Ωch² = 0.120, i.e. Ωdm ≈ 0.27.

  • Dark Matter, Rotation Curves, and the Morphology of Galaxies ↗
    2021 arXiv:2108.13350

    Links dark-matter content to galaxy morphology across the rotation-curve sample.
